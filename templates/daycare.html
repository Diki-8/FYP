Dog Daycare at Paws Home

A safe, fun, and nurturing environment where your dog can play, socialize, and thrive while you're away.

Why Choose Our Daycare?

At Paws Home, we understand that your dog is family. Our daycare service is designed to provide physical activity, mental stimulation, and socialization in a carefully supervised setting.

Safe & Secure Environment

Our facility features secure play areas, climate-controlled spaces, and clean facilities to ensure your dog's comfort and safety at all times.

Structured Play & Activities

We balance active playtime with rest periods, including group play (for social dogs), individual attention, and engaging toys that promote healthy exercise.

Professional Supervision

Our trained staff monitors all interactions, maintains small group sizes, and follows strict health protocols to prevent overstimulation or stress.

Socialization Benefits

Regular daycare helps dogs develop confidence, learn proper social behavior, and reduce separation anxiety through positive interactions.

Flexible Scheduling

Open weekdays with extended hours to accommodate working pet parents. We also offer half-day options for puppies or senior dogs.

Is Daycare Right for Your Dog?

Daycare is ideal for:

  • Active dogs that need daytime exercise
  • Puppies learning socialization skills (after completing vaccinations)
  • Dogs with separation anxiety who benefit from companionship
  • Pet owners with long work hours or irregular schedules

All dogs must pass a temperament evaluation and provide proof of vaccinations for the safety of all our guests.
