About Us

Welcome to Paws Home – A Trusted Dog Boarding Service.

What is Dog Boarding?

Dog boarding, also known as dog daycare, allows pet owners to leave their dogs at a trusted facility for a specific time. While common in developed countries, this concept is still emerging in Nepal. Our goal is to bridge this gap by providing a safe and well-managed space for dogs.

Our Vision

We want to redefine dog care in Nepal by offering a professional and trustworthy boarding service. Our aim is to ensure every dog receives proper care, attention, and a homely environment while their owners are occupied.
